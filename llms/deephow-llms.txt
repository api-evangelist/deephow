# DeepHow

> DeepHow is an enterprise Physical AI platform that helps industrial companies capture, verify, and optimize operational knowledge across manufacturing, pharmaceutical, and electronics assembly environments. It turns expert know-how into searchable digital workflows, uses vision AI for live standard-operating-procedure verification and on-demand visual inspection, and applies time-and-motion AI for task analysis and operator training.

DeepHow is used by 100+ enterprises including Stanley Black & Decker, Anheuser-Busch InBev, Foxconn, and GSK. This profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network. DeepHow does not currently publish a public developer API or API reference; the entries below point at the company's public product, support, and policy surfaces.
